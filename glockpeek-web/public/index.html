Overview

Composite health

Over all recorded history (not the selected window): 100 minus penalties for unmanaged time, violations, and deliberate days. Higher is healthier.

Data sync

When the glocker daemon last pushed records into glockpeek, and what the last batch carried.

History calendar

Each cell is a day. Green = clean & managed, red = violations, hatched = unmanaged (glocker off). Click a day for details.

Timeline

Violations per day across the window. Click a point for details.

Day detail

Vulnerability map

Violations by hour × weekday. Brighter is more exposed.

Ignored violations

Hits you marked “not a violation” from a day's detail. They’re hidden from every count; restore any to bring it back. The raw log is never modified.

Activity per day

Active time each day in the window, stacked by tag. The label above each bar is the day's total active time and its share of the 24-hour day.

Time by tag

Active time recategorized by your rules. Untagged time is shown so you can see coverage. Click a tag to see what fed it.

Tag activity by hour of day

Active hours in each hour-of-day slot (00–23), stacked by tag.

Tag contributors

Click a tag in “Time by tag” above, then click an app to expand its windows and see its common title words.

Common words
click an app to see its common title words

Untagged windows

Top 10 individual windows no rule matched, by active time. Click + tag to start a rule for that window, then set a tag below and Save.

Rules

First rule whose program & title regexes both match wins. Blank = match anything. Saved as JSON; raw log untouched.
